# v0.12.0 — TV channels made from your own library

This release turns your Plex library into TV channels. The app suggests channels it thinks you will like, previews what each would be airing right now, and keeps the ones you pick in the Live TV guide, with or without a Tunarr server. It also fixes distorted Live TV audio and a Continue Watching row that put the wrong show first.

## What's new

- **Suggested channels.** Home has a new **Suggested Channels** row, and Live TV has a **Channels** page. The suggestions come from what this profile watches, the time of day, the day of the week and the season: a channel built around a show you love, the decade and genre you watch most, a network, a director, a collection, shows you are part-way through, gems you have not seen, a Saturday morning of cartoons, horror in October. Each one says why it is suggested and how much it holds. **Surprise Me** draws one from a corner of your library you might not think of.
- **See a channel before you keep it.** Opening a suggestion shows what it would be airing at this moment, how far in it is, and what comes next. **Reshuffle** deals a new order (and always puts something else on now), **Order** switches between shuffled, in rotation, in blocks and in order, and **Edit** narrows it to films or shows, to what you have not watched, or to a rating ceiling, with the count updating as you go. **Not Interested** hides a suggestion for good.
- **Make your own channels.** Hold OK on a show, season, collection or playlist and choose **Make a Channel** to preview a channel of just that, then keep it. Or choose **New Channel** on the Channels page and build one from your library: pick a genre, a decade, films or shows, unwatched only, a rating ceiling. Each choice only offers what your library actually has, the count of films, shows and hours updates as you go, and the channel names itself ("90s Sitcoms"). A channel built this way keeps up with your library: what you add later joins it.
- **Keep a channel and it joins the guide.** **Keep Channel** gives it a number from 900 up. It shows in the guide, in On Now on Home, and between channels with CH▲ / CH▼, like any other channel. It is saved as a playlist on your Plex server, with a TV mark at the start of its title, so every television in the house that runs this app sees the same channels. Delete it from the Channels page, or delete the playlist in any Plex app.
- **Watching a channel is like watching TV.** Tuning one joins whatever is airing, partway through, and the next programme follows on its own. Pausing falls behind live; the banner then says **BEHIND**, and OK jumps back to live. Watching a channel does not mark anything watched and does not touch Continue Watching.
- **Live TV no longer needs Tunarr.** With no Tunarr server, the Live TV tab appears as soon as your library can suggest a channel, and the guide shows the channels you keep.

## Fixed

- **Live TV audio sounded distorted.** A channel now buffers more of both its picture and its sound before it starts, so the sound no longer starves at the start of a channel.
- **Continue Watching put the wrong show first.** After you finished an episode and started the next, the show could appear further down the row. The row now keeps the order your server gives it, and is read again a few seconds after you stop.
- **On the Live TV page, the top bar highlighted Movies.** The highlight now sits on Live TV.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — none for this version.** This version has not yet been run on a television. The channel pages have been checked on a computer against a stand-in Plex server; watching a channel and the audio fix have not been seen on a set.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.12.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

If your set is in the second group, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

- **A kept channel is saved as a visible playlist.** It appears among your playlists in every Plex app, its title starting with a TV mark. Deleting it there deletes the channel.
- **A channel draws from at most 4,000 programmes.** A very large library is sampled to keep the schedule quick to build on the television.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.12.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.12.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. This package bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and its complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.12.0](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.12.0.md).
