# v0.11.0 — A richer Live TV guide, and a Plex-sized feature catch-up

The Live TV guide gains channel logos, artwork, genre colours and a genre filter, and the app gains the Plex features it was missing: your Watchlist, playlists, shuffle, a play queue, a version picker, automatic intro and credit skipping, more library filters, and a smarter Home.

## What's new

- **A guide that looks like a guide.** Each channel shows its logo (or its initials on a colour of its own), and the top of the guide shows the highlighted programme's artwork, episode, times, how long is left and its description. Programmes carry a coloured edge by genre, what is on now says how many minutes are left, and the part already aired is shaded. Logos and artwork are kept on the television, so the guide shows them at once next time.
- **Filter the guide by genre.** Press Up from the top row to reach a strip of genres (Movies, Sports, Kids, News and the guide's own categories). Programmes that do not match are dimmed and channels with nothing matching are hidden.
- **Watch a programme from the start in Plex.** When the highlighted programme is also in your Plex library, the guide says so: hold OK to open it there and watch it from the beginning. A normal press still tunes the channel.
- **On Now on Home.** With Live TV set up, Home has a row of what is on each channel, the one you watched last first. OK tunes it.
- **Your Plex Watchlist.** Home shows the titles on your watchlist that your libraries have. Add or remove a movie or show from its page or from a card's menu (hold OK).
- **Playlists.** Your video playlists appear on Home. Open one to see what is in it, and press Play to watch it in order.
- **Shuffle.** A show's page and the menu on a show or season card offer Shuffle. Every episode after the first, Up Next included, comes from the same shuffle.
- **The play queue.** While something plays, More → Play queue lists what is before and after it. OK on a row jumps to it.
- **Choose a version.** When a movie or episode has several versions (a 4K HDR copy and a 1080p one), its page has a Version button listing them, and More → Version in the player switches without losing your place.
- **Skip intro & credits by itself.** Settings → Playback → Skip intro & credits can skip intros, or intros and credits, as soon as they start. For five seconds afterwards a Back button (or Left) returns to where it skipped from. Credits are never skipped while Up Next counts down to the next episode. It is off unless you turn it on.
- **More library filters.** A library's Filter menu adds what your server offers among year, decade, resolution, HDR, content rating, director, actor and studio. Filters combine, and the Filter button names the ones in force.
- **A smarter Home.** Continue Watching now refreshes after anything you watch, when you come back to Home, when the app returns to the screen and every two minutes while Home is open, without the row jumping. It also leaves out things with nothing left to continue. A new row, Recently Added in Your Genres, shows new titles in the genres you watch most.
- **Picks up where you left off.** If you opened the app in the last 12 hours, it starts on Home and then returns to the library, Search, Live TV or the page of what you last played. Press any button while it starts to stay on Home. A different profile always starts on Home.
- **A screensaver.** After five minutes with nothing pressed on a browsing page, the screen fades to a slow slideshow of artwork from your library with the time. Any button brings the page back without pressing anything on it. It never starts while video plays. Settings → Playback → Screensaver changes the wait or turns it off.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — none for this version.** This version has not yet been run on a television. Everything new was checked in the desktop simulator against a stand-in Plex server and a stand-in Tunarr.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

## Known issues

- **A Tunarr channel whose frame rate changes partway through can stutter or stop.** The rate is read once when you tune. In Tunarr, turn on **Normalize frame rate** in the transcode settings so each channel keeps one rate.
- **The clock and the guide's times can be off by your time zone.** Some televisions do not tell apps their time zone. When the app sees UTC it asks the television's own clock service, and if that does not answer it uses the time zone your Tunarr server writes into its guide.
- **Shuffle, the play queue, playlists and the Watchlist have only been tried against a stand-in server.** They follow Plex's published interface; if one misbehaves with your server, please report it.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.11.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV. It installs over v0.10.0 and keeps your sign-in and settings.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.11.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. This package bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and its complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.11.0](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md).
