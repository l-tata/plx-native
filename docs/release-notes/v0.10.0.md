# v0.10.0 — Live TV channels play

Live TV channels from Tunarr now play. In v0.9.0 a channel tuned, Tunarr started its stream, and the picture never came: the player stayed on Buffering.

## Fixed

- **A Live TV channel stayed on Buffering and never showed a picture.** The app read Tunarr's video and audio as if they came from a film file and handed the TV's decoder data it could not play. Channels now play as Tunarr sends them, including a channel you join in the middle of a programme.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — none for this version.** This version has not yet been run on a television. The fix was checked on a computer against video made the way Tunarr makes it.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.10.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

## Known issues

- **A Tunarr channel whose frame rate changes partway through can stutter or stop.** The rate is read once when you tune. In Tunarr, turn on **Normalize frame rate** in the transcode settings so each channel keeps one rate.
- **The clock and the guide's times can be off by your time zone.** Some televisions do not tell apps their time zone. When the app sees UTC it asks the television's own clock service, and if that does not answer it uses the time zone your Tunarr server writes into its guide.

## Help test this release

**If you run Tunarr, tell us whether channels play now.** Changing channel, the channel list (Up / Down while a channel plays) and whether the picture and sound stay in step are the things nobody has seen on a television yet. [Open an issue](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.10.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV. It installs over v0.9.0 and keeps your sign-in and settings.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.10.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. This package bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and its complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.10.0](https://github.com/l-tata/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.10.0.md).
